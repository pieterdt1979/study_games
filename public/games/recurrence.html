🔢 Recurrence Detective

How to Play

You're given the first several terms of a sequence. Identify the recurrence relation, predict the next term, and (in advanced mode) solve the recurrence by finding the characteristic equation roots. The sequence might be linear, Fibonacci-type, or inhomogeneous!

Theory

A linear recurrence: aₙ = c₁aₙ₋₁ + c₂aₙ₋₂ + ...

To solve: substitute aₙ = αⁿ to get the characteristic equation. If roots are α₁, α₂: aₙ = A·α₁ⁿ + B·α₂ⁿ. Find A,B from initial conditions.
