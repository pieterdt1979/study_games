✏️ Planarity Puzzle

How to Play

Drag vertices to rearrange the graph so that no two edges cross. If the graph is planar, you can always untangle it! If it's non-planar (contains a K₅ or K₃,₃ configuration), click "Non-Planar!" to declare it impossible. Use Euler's formula corollary: if e > 3v - 6, the graph CANNOT be planar.

Theory

A graph is planar if it can be drawn without edge crossings. Euler's formula: r = e - v + 2 (regions = edges - vertices + 2). Corollary: if e > 3v - 6, the graph is non-planar. For triangle-free graphs: e > 2v - 4 means non-planar.
