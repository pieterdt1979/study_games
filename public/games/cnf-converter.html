How to Play

Convert a CFG to Chomsky Normal Form step by step. Each step builds on the previous. The three steps (in order): 1. Eliminate Λ-productions, 2. Eliminate unit productions, 3. Rewrite in CNF form (A→BC or A→a).

CNF Rules

          

            CNF form: Every production must be either:

            • A → BC (two nonterminals)

            • A → a (one terminal)


            Steps:

            1. Find nullable NTs, add replacement productions, remove Λ-prods

            2. Remove unit prods (A→B), replace with B's productions

            3. Introduce new NTs for terminals in mixed prods, split long RHS
