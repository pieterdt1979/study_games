Theory

A PDA = FA + pushdown stack. It can:
• READ input (one char at a time)
• PUSH onto stack
• POP from stack

PDAs accept exactly the context-free languages.
Nondeterministic PDAs > Deterministic PDAs (unlike FAs!).
