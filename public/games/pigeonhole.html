🐦 Pigeonhole Principle

How to Play

The Pigeonhole Principle states: if n items are placed into m containers with n > m, then at least one container must hold more than one item. Apply this to solve guaranteed-outcome problems!

The Principle

Basic form: If n pigeons go into m holes and n > m, some hole has ≥ 2 pigeons.

Generalized: If n items go into m containers, some container has ≥ ⌈n/m⌉ items.

For "minimum draws": To GUARANTEE at least k items of one type from m types, you need:
(k−1)×m + 1 draws in the worst case.

Key insight: The principle is about the WORST case, not average or best case. You must assume maximum bad luck.
