How to Play

The circle-chord method proves a graph is non-planar. Steps: (1) Find a Hamilton circuit and draw it as a circle. (2) The remaining edges become chords. (3) Place each chord inside or outside the circle. (4) If two chords on the same side cross, they conflict. If ALL placements lead to conflict, the graph is non-planar!

Circle-Chord Method

1. Draw Hamilton circuit as circle
2. Remaining edges are chords
3. Two chords conflict if placed on same side AND their endpoints alternate on the circle (they must cross)
4. Chord ab and cd conflict on the same side iff their endpoints appear in order a...c...b...d around the circle
5. If no valid placement exists → NON-PLANAR
