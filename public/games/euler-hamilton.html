How to Play

Click vertices to build a path. In Euler mode, traverse every edge exactly once. In Hamilton mode, visit every vertex exactly once and return to start. Right-click to undo the last step.

Theory

A connected multigraph has an Euler cycle iff all vertices have even degree. It has an Euler trail (but no cycle) iff exactly 2 vertices have odd degree.
