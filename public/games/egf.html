🔬 Exponential Generating Functions

How to Play

Build exponential generating functions for arrangements of distinct objects. The answer aᵣ is the coefficient of xʳ/r! (not xʳ). Use the power series for eˣ, cosh x, and sinh x to find coefficients.

Key Series

eˣ = Σ xʳ/r! (coeff of xʳ/r! = 1)

e²ˣ = Σ 2ʳ·xʳ/r! (coeff = 2ʳ)

cosh x = (eˣ+e⁻ˣ)/2 = Σ x²ⁿ/(2n)!
(even powers only)

sinh x = (eˣ−e⁻ˣ)/2 = Σ x²ⁿ⁺¹/(2n+1)!
(odd powers only)

Important: aᵣ = coefficient of xʳ/r!, NOT of xʳ.
If coeff of xʳ is B, then aᵣ = B·r!

EGF vs OGF

OGF (ordinary): for selections/identical objects
EGF (exponential): for arrangements/distinct objects

Each "container" contributes a factor. If container allows 0,1,2,... items: factor = eˣ.
Even count only: cosh x.
Odd count only: sinh x.
At most k: 1+x+x²/2!+...+xᵏ/k!
